返回。</p> <br><p> </p> <br><p><strong>示例 1：</strong></p> <br><pre><strong>输入：</strong>s = "()())()"<br><strong>输出：</strong>["(())()","()()()"]<br></pre> <br><p><strong>示例 2：</strong></p> <br><pre><strong>输入：</strong>s = "(a)())()"<br><strong>输出：</strong>["(a())()","(a)()()"]<br></pre> <br><p><strong>示例 3：</strong></p> <br><pre><strong>输入：</strong>s =
