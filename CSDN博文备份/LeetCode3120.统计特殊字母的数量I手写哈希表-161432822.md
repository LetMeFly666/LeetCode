rel="nofollow">https://leetcode.cn/problems/count-the-number-of-special-characters-i/</a></p> <br><p>给你一个字符串 <code>word</code>。如果 <code>word</code> 中同时存在某个字母的小写形式和大写形式，则称这个字母为 <strong>特殊字母</strong> 。</p> <br><p>返回 <code>word</code> 中<strong> </strong><strong>特殊字母 </strong>的数量。</p> <br><p> </p> <br><p><strong class="example">示例 1:</strong></p> <br><div class="example-block"> <br> <p><strong>输入：</strong><span class="example-io">word = "aaAbcBC"</span></p> <br></div><br><p><strong>输出：</strong><span class="example-io">3</span></p> <br><p><strong>解释：</strong></p> <p><strong>输入：</strong><span class="example-io">word = "abc"</span></p> <br></div><br><p><strong>输出：</strong><span class="example-io">0</span></p> <br><p><strong>解释：</strong></p> <br><p><code>word</code> 中不存在大小写形式同时出现的字母。</p> <br><p><strong class="example">示例 3:</strong></p> <br><div class="example-block"> <br> <p><strong>输入：</strong><span class="example-io">word =
